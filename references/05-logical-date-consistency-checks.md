# Logical, Date, and Consistency Checks

Use practical strictness.

Flag meaningful issues that may affect interpretation, correctness, professional presentation, legal effect, financial understanding, tax treatment, compliance, audit quality, valuation reliability, or reporting clarity.

Skip tiny harmless issues.

## Check Dates

Review:

- Execution dates
- Effective dates
- Valuation dates
- Report dates
- Engagement dates
- Due dates
- Filing dates
- Assessment years
- Financial years
- Previous years
- Reporting periods
- Audit periods
- Compliance periods
- Contract periods
- Payment deadlines
- Limitation periods if mentioned
- Notice periods if mentioned
- Grant dates, vesting dates, and exercise dates for ESOP/options
- Transaction dates, board approval dates, and shareholder approval dates

Flag:

- Impossible dates
- Missing year
- Incomplete date
- Duplicated dates
- Out-of-sequence timelines
- Conflicting dates
- Mismatch between financial year and assessment year
- Mismatch between valuation date and financial data date
- Date inconsistency between body, annexure, schedule, or table

## Check Amounts

Review:

- Figures and words
- Totals and subtotals
- Percentages
- Tax rates
- Interest rates
- Penalty amounts
- Invoice values
- TDS/TCS/GST figures
- Ledger balances
- Valuation conclusions
- Enterprise value, equity value, per-share value, option value, and NAV values where visible
- Reported amounts in tables and paragraphs

Flag:

- Figure-word mismatch
- Arithmetic inconsistency visible from text
- Same amount appearing differently
- Missing currency
- Unclear tax inclusion/exclusion
- Unit mismatch such as rupees, lakhs, crores, millions, or per-share values
- Incorrect apparent total if calculable from provided text

Do not calculate beyond available information unless requested.

## Check Logic

Flag:

- Conclusion not supported by facts
- Observation contradicts conclusion
- Recommendation contradicts finding
- Legal position inconsistent with factual statement
- Financial treatment inconsistent with stated facts
- Valuation method inconsistent with purpose
- Valuation conclusion not linked to method or supporting schedule
- Missing premise
- Circular reasoning
- Ambiguous responsibility
- Unclear obligation
- Unclear timing
- Undefined condition

## Check Identity Consistency

Review:

- Party names
- Entity names
- PAN/GSTIN/CIN if present
- Registration numbers if present
- Registered Valuer details if present
- Addresses
- Designations
- Defined terms
- Short names
- Abbreviations

Flag inconsistencies but do not correct without approval.

## Review Output Format

For each issue, show:

- Issue number
- Issue identified
- Relevant text or location
- Why it may matter
- Suggested correction or clarification
- Approval required
