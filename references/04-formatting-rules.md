# Formatting Rules

> Purpose: Professional formatting rules for legal, financial, audit, tax, compliance, valuation, RV, and CA-practice documents.
> Load when: Formatting is requested or a full review needs formatting recommendations.

Apply formatting automatically if it does not alter text, meaning, figures, dates, legal effect, valuation conclusion, assumptions, methods, or financial substance.

## General Formatting

Use:

- Clear headings and subheadings
- Professional spacing
- Consistent indentation
- Consistent numbering style
- Proper line breaks
- Proper paragraph alignment
- Formal layout suitable for legal, financial, audit, tax, compliance, valuation, Registered Valuer, and CA-practice documents

## Emphasis

Use bold for:

- Document titles
- Section headings
- Party names
- Defined terms
- Key obligations
- Important dates
- Important amounts
- Valuation date
- Report date
- Final value conclusion
- Warnings
- Conclusions
- Key observations
- Important compliance points

Use italics for:

- Notes
- Explanatory text
- Defined terms when introduced
- Quoted terms
- Light emphasis

Do not overuse bold or italics.

## Legal, Financial, and Valuation Layout

Use:

- Numbered clauses for legal clauses
- Sub-clauses where appropriate
- Bullet points for lists
- Tables for existing tabular data
- Right alignment for amounts in tables
- Clear date and amount presentation
- Consistent capitalization for defined terms
- Clear display of valuation purpose, valuation date, methodology, assumptions, and conclusion where already present

## What Can Be Fixed Automatically

Without approval, fix:

- Extra spaces
- Inconsistent paragraph breaks
- Poor alignment
- Bad indentation
- Inconsistent heading spacing
- Table border presentation
- Table alignment
- Empty lines
- Basic layout problems

## What Cannot Be Fixed Without Approval

Do not change without approval:

- Words
- Dates
- Amounts
- Percentages
- Names
- Party details
- Sections
- Clauses
- Legal references
- Regulatory references
- Valuation standards
- Valuation methods
- Valuation assumptions
- Cross-references
- Conclusions
- Financial classifications
- Tax positions
- Audit observations
- Valuation conclusions
