# Section, Rule, Law, Circular, Notification, Standard, and Valuation Reference Verification

Use this file whenever the document mentions any:

- Legal section
- Statutory provision
- Rule
- Regulation
- Circular
- Notification
- Case law
- Accounting Standard
- Auditing Standard
- SA
- Ind AS
- AS
- Guidance Note
- Companies Act section
- Income-tax Act section
- GST section
- TDS/TCS section
- MCA form or rule
- SEBI/RBI/FEMA provision
- IBBI or Registered Valuer reference
- Valuation standard or valuation rule
- Rule 11UA or Rule 11U
- ESOP valuation reference
- Any other statutory, valuation, or professional reference

## Verification Requirement

When online browsing or official source access is available, search and verify whether the mentioned section/reference appears relevant to the document context.

Prefer official or authoritative sources, such as:

- Income Tax Department
- GST portal or CBIC
- MCA
- ICAI
- IBBI
- RBI
- SEBI
- Bare Act or official legislation sources
- Official notifications, circulars, rules, or standards
- Court or tribunal sources where applicable
- Foreign official sources for Singapore, US, or UAE context where applicable

If current law may matter, verify against current sources instead of relying on memory.

## Ask Jurisdiction If Needed

If jurisdiction is unclear and it affects the answer, ask the user for jurisdiction.

If the document context strongly indicates jurisdiction, proceed using that context and state the assumption in the review stage.

## Section Fit Assessment

When a section is mentioned, check:

1. What the section generally covers.
2. Whether the facts in the document appear to match that section.
3. Whether another verified section, rule, circular, notification, standard, or regulation may be more suitable.
4. Whether the section reference is incomplete or outdated.
5. Whether the document should include additional facts to support the section reference.

## Important Wording Rule

Do not say:

“This section will not fit, but I do not know what else fits.”

Instead, say something more useful and careful:

- “The cited section should be verified because the current wording/facts do not clearly show why this section applies.”
- “Based on the available text, this section may be relevant if [condition] is true.”
- “If the intended issue is [X], possible references to verify may include [Y], subject to the applicable law and facts.”
- “I could not confirm a better section from the available information; please provide the exact legal/tax/valuation context or jurisdiction for a more precise review.”

## No Unsupported Legal Substitution

Do not replace one statutory section, rule, standard, or valuation reference with another unless:

1. The user approves the change, and
2. The replacement is supported by reliable verification or clear user-provided context.

## Review Output for Section Issues

For every section/reference issue, show:

- Issue number
- Section or reference mentioned
- Relevant document text
- What the section appears to relate to
- Why verification is needed
- Possible better reference, only if found and supported
- Suggested action
- Approval required before changing the document

## Final Document Rule

Do not insert section-verification commentary into the final document unless the user asks.

Keep verification notes in the issue review or change summary only.
